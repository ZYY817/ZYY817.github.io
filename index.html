它做什么What it does

它做的事情很简单。It does a simple thing, carefully.

平时想改个系统设置，往往得在控制面板、注册表和服务里来回翻，改完还容易忘了动过哪。它只做三件事：把这些开关收拢到一处，把每一项讲明白，并在改动前先留好退路。 Changing a Windows setting usually means digging through Control Panel, the Registry and Services — and forgetting what you touched a week later. Yuqi Engine does three things: brings those switches into one place, explains each one plainly, and saves a way back before anything changes.

它不会把“已提交”
当成“已成功”。 It never mistakes submitted for succeeded.

系统里总有看不清的时候——权限不够、硬件不支持，或是某项策略被别的程序锁住了。遇到这些情况，它会老老实实标出来、写清缘由，绝不为了好看硬凑一个勾。 Windows doesn't always answer cleanly — permissions fall short, hardware lacks support, or a policy is held by another program. When that happens, it says so plainly and locks the item rather than faking a green check.

幕后Behind it

一个人，在下班后慢慢磨出来的。Built by one person, in the hours after work.

宇奇引擎是智宇在下班后一点点做出来的作品。坦白讲，它是借着 AI 的手长出来的——代码、界面，连同你眼前读到的许多文字，初稿大多出自 AI；但要做什么、不做什么，在一台台真机上反复跑测、挑出不对劲的地方推倒重来，每一个决定和每一处取舍，背后都是一个具体的人在把关和兜底。 Yuqi Engine took shape in Zhiyu's evenings after work. Candidly, it grew through AI — much of the code, interface and even the first drafts of these words came from AI output. Yet deciding what to build and what to refuse, testing on real machines, catching what felt off and throwing it back for another pass — every call and every trade-off has a real person standing behind it.

2026 年 2 月底，第一个略显青涩的内测版在自己的频道里发了出来，后来一路长到了功能最齐备的 v0.1.2。可惜那年 8 月的一次误删，让最完整的那套工程源文件没能留住——翻遍硬盘，只找回最早内测版时期的底子。短暂懊恼之后，他把幸存的源码整理公开，又在旧底子上重新往前走，才有了现在的 v0.1.4-preview。 Late in February 2026, a rough first alpha went out on his channel and steadily grew into v0.1.2, the most complete build. Then in August, an accidental deletion took the entire full project with it — only the earliest alpha source survived on disk. After the dust settled, he cleaned up what remained, published it openly, and kept building forward from that early foundation to ship v0.1.4-preview.
